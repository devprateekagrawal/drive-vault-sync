Privacy Policy: Drive Vault Sync

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Drive Vault Sync is an Obsidian plugin that copies your vault's files between your devices and a folder in your own Google Drive.

What data it accesses

With your permission, the plugin uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. This lets it create, read, update and delete only the files and folders it created itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

Where your data goes

  • Your notes travel only between your own devices and your own Google Drive, directly over Google's API.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any other third party. There is no analytics or tracking.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on each of your devices, in the plugin's local settings file.
  • The sign-in redirect page (/auth/) only forwards Google's one-time code to the Obsidian app on your device. It stores and sends nothing.

Use of Google user data

Drive Vault Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out in the plugin settings, or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files stay in your Drive until you delete them.
