Drive Vault Sync

A free Obsidian plugin that keeps your vault in sync between your devices through your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Two-way sync of notes, folders and attachments between Obsidian on desktop and mobile.
  • Your notes are stored in a folder in your own Google Drive. There is no server in between.
  • It uses the limited drive.file permission, so it can only see the files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive.
